  • Developed a Rust/WebAssembly gateway that connects to local execution hosts over WebSocket. Hosts advertise available agents, and the gateway dispatches tasks according to tenant and device bindings.
  • Implemented task deduplication and reconnect recovery. Hosts persist tasks and pending results in SQLite, then resend unacknowledged events after reconnecting so a lost acknowledgment does not trigger duplicate execution.
